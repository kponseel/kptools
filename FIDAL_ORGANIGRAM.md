- Already integrated with **LexisNexis Lexis PolyOffice** and **Septeo**
- **eIDAS compliant** (Simple + Advanced + Qualified)
- **International** coverage for Unyer/WTS Global network
- **Azure-native SaaS** = aligns with Fidal's cloud strategy
- **Enterprise security** = satisfies CISO Mariotto
- **Proven in French law firms**: Scotto & Associes, Linklaters, Pomelaw, Agil'IT
